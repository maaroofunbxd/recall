# Journal

## 2019-06-11

Warm. Took the long way home past the market. Bought peaches, they were not
good. The man at the stall said they would be better next week. He was right.

## 2019-11-02

Rained all day. Stayed in and read. Made soup with too much salt.

## 2020-06-11

Cold for June. Worked late and missed dinner. A. called and we talked for an
hour about nothing much.

## 2021-03-28

Walked to the sea. Windy. Sat on the wall for a while and watched the ferry
come in. Ate a sandwich that was mostly bread.

## 2021-09-14

Bus was late so I walked. Saw the same dog outside the same shop as last
week. Someone had chalked a hopscotch grid on the pavement.

## 2022-06-11

Hot. Bought a fan. The shop had none left so I carried one across town from
the other place. It was worth it.
